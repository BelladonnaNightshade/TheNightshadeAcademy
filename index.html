Department of Etiquette

Professor Elaine Veinmore

Young Vampiress

Cheerful, youthful, and endlessly enthusiastic, Professor Elaine Veinmore teaches young monsters the finer points of poise, grace, restraint, and refined menace.

Elaine absolutely adores Headmistress Belladonna—perhaps a little too much—and rarely misses an opportunity to quote her. Despite teaching flawless composure, she is famously clumsy. Teacups spill, demonstrations unravel, and furniture has learned to fear her. She always recovers with a smile and insists that true elegance lies in how one survives embarrassment.

Teaches Poise, formal conduct, social menace, ceremonial etiquette
Known for Encouragement, Belladonna devotion, accidental catastrophes
Classroom reputation Warm, lively, and one overturned tea service from disaster
“Elegance is not never falling. It is rising before anyone has time to laugh.”

Professor Veinmore in Practice

Composure is sometimes a recovery exercise.

Her lessons are impeccable. Her demonstrations are memorable for other reasons.

Department of History and Ancient Magic

Professor Mortimer Blackbriar

Ancient Lich

An ancient lich of immense age and unimpeachable scholarship, Professor Mortimer Blackbriar presides over the study of history, relics, and the oldest surviving forms of magic.

Mortimer insists he has long since “transcended fleshly distractions,” and carries himself with grave dignity befitting a scholar who remembers empires most people only read about. Belladonna considers Nightshade Academy fortunate to have such an esteemed authority. His colleagues agree on his brilliance, though they privately nurse a less dignified title for him: “The Bone Daddy.” Never to his face.

Teaches Ancient civilizations, forbidden history, relic interpretation, pre-mortal sorcery
Known for Monumental knowledge, grave self-importance, and a baffling indifference to romance
Classroom reputation Exacting, mesmerizing, and old enough to correct the dead on their own history
“History does not repeat itself. It merely waits for the living to make the same mistakes.”

Among the Faculty

Respect, awe, and one forbidden nickname.

Mortimer assumes the hush that follows him is reverence. In truth, the staff are usually trying not to laugh at a nickname they treasure far too much to risk saying aloud.

Department of Toxic Botany and Evil Plants

Professor Paisley Thistledoom

Poisonwood Dryad

A Poisonwood Dryad of rare refinement, Professor Paisley Thistledoom governs the Department of Toxic Botany and Evil Plants with perfect composure and deeply questionable maternal instincts.

In her cultivated humanoid form she is poised, cheerful, and almost entirely absorbed by plant life. She is frequently hilarious, though rarely on purpose. Her affection for deadly flora is sincere, maternal, and more than a little disturbing; she speaks to carnivorous plants as though they were gifted but temperamental children. When provoked, she may unfurl into a far older shape of roots, thorns, venomous blossoms, and predatory vines.

Teaches Poisonous flora, carnivorous cultivation, venomous pollens, and botanical containment
Known for Forgetting students’ names, remembering every plant, and encouraging dangerous growth with alarming tenderness
Classroom reputation Warm, fascinating, and potentially fatal—especially if something appears to be asleep
“Oh, she isn’t trying to eat you. She is only testing whether you are nutritious.”

When Provoked

The dreadful shape beneath the botanist.

Most students adore Professor Thistledoom right up until the greenhouse reveals that she is, in fact, the most dangerous plant in it.
