Founder · Headmistress · Vampire Queen

Headmistress Belladonna Nightshade

The authority behind every polished terror, every accepted disaster, and every standard the Academy refuses to lower.

Belladonna Nightshade founded The Nightshade Academy to transform raw monstrosity into elegance, discipline, and distinguished dread.

As Headmistress, she oversees every department, approves every admission, and intervenes whenever a student’s potential becomes either exceptional or catastrophically interesting. She believes power without discipline is merely noise—and fear without elegance is forgettable.

Position Founder and Headmistress of The Nightshade Academy

Known for Absolute authority, impossible standards, refined menace, and knowing when students lie
“My students will not merely become monsters. They will become legends whispered about correctly.”

The Esteemed Faculty

The Faculty of The Nightshade Academy

Select a professor to learn more about the scholars shaping Nightshade Academy’s most refined horrors.

Department of Etiquette

Professor Elaine Veinmore

Young Vampiress

Cheerful, youthful, and endlessly enthusiastic, Professor Elaine Veinmore teaches young monsters the finer points of poise, grace, restraint, and refined menace.

Elaine absolutely adores Headmistress Belladonna—perhaps a little too much—and rarely misses an opportunity to quote her. Despite teaching flawless composure, she is famously clumsy. Teacups spill, demonstrations unravel, and furniture has learned to fear her. She always recovers with a smile and insists that true elegance lies in how one survives embarrassment.

Teaches Poise, formal conduct, social menace, ceremonial etiquette
Known for Encouragement, Belladonna devotion, accidental catastrophes
Classroom reputation Warm, lively, and one overturned tea service from disaster
“Elegance is not never falling. It is rising before anyone has time to laugh.”

Professor Veinmore in Practice

Composure is sometimes a recovery exercise.

Her lessons are impeccable. Her demonstrations are memorable for other reasons.

Department of History and Ancient Magic

Professor Mortimer Blackbriar

Ancient Lich

An ancient lich of immense age and unimpeachable scholarship, Professor Mortimer Blackbriar presides over the study of history, relics, and the oldest surviving forms of magic.

Mortimer insists he has long since “transcended fleshly distractions,” and carries himself with grave dignity befitting a scholar who remembers empires most people only read about. Belladonna considers Nightshade Academy fortunate to have such an esteemed authority. His colleagues agree on his brilliance, though they privately nurse a less dignified title for him: “The Bone Daddy.” Never to his face.

Teaches Ancient civilizations, forbidden history, relic interpretation, pre-mortal sorcery
Known for Monumental knowledge, grave self-importance, and a baffling indifference to romance
Classroom reputation Exacting, mesmerizing, and old enough to correct the dead on their own history
“History does not repeat itself. It merely waits for the living to make the same mistakes.”

Among the Faculty

Respect, awe, and one forbidden nickname.

Mortimer assumes the hush that follows him is reverence. In truth, the staff are usually trying not to laugh at a nickname they treasure far too much to risk saying aloud.

Department of Toxic Botany and Evil Plants

Professor Paisley Thistledoom

Poisonwood Dryad

A Poisonwood Dryad of rare refinement, Professor Paisley Thistledoom governs the Department of Toxic Botany and Evil Plants with perfect composure and deeply questionable maternal instincts.

In her cultivated humanoid form she is poised, cheerful, and almost entirely absorbed by plant life. She is frequently hilarious, though rarely on purpose. Her affection for deadly flora is sincere, maternal, and more than a little disturbing; she speaks to carnivorous plants as though they were gifted but temperamental children. When provoked, she may unfurl into a far older shape of roots, thorns, venomous blossoms, and predatory vines.

Teaches Poisonous flora, carnivorous cultivation, venomous pollens, and botanical containment
Known for Forgetting students’ names, remembering every plant, and encouraging dangerous growth with alarming tenderness
Classroom reputation Warm, fascinating, and potentially fatal—especially if something appears to be asleep
“Oh, she isn’t trying to eat you. She is only testing whether you are nutritious.”

When Provoked

The dreadful shape beneath the botanist.

Most students adore Professor Thistledoom right up until the greenhouse reveals that she is, in fact, the most dangerous plant in it.

Department of Alchemy and Magical Transformations

Dr. Felix Mire

Alchemical Doppelganger

Created in an experimental alchemical vessel, Dr. Felix Mire is a brilliant, charming, and dangerously enthusiastic authority on transformation.

Felix was formed from unstable mutagenic substances and traces of several living donors. His body never settled into one permanent shape, so he learned to sculpt it himself. The polished appearance he presents today is carefully maintained, but excitement, fear, distraction, or volatile magic can cause fragments of other forms to slip through. He regards explosions, mutations, and “unfortunate results” as valuable research—especially the explosions.

TeachesAlchemy, magical transformation, unstable mutagens, transmutation, and experimental magical chemistry
Known forQuestionable experiments, shifting features, cheerful recklessness, and a genuine love of explosions
Classroom reputationBrilliant, exhilarating, and safest when observed from behind reinforced glass
“If it explodes, transforms, or develops extra teeth, we have learned something valuable.”

Native State

A body made of possibilities.

Without the shape he consciously maintains, Felix becomes a living alchemical mixture in which borrowed faces, eyes, and anatomies surface and vanish beneath translucent flesh.

Department of Omens and Prophecy

Professor Lorna Cawthorne

Raventic Harpy

Elegant, severe, and professionally nosy, Professor Lorna Cawthorne sees nearly everything that happens at Nightshade Academy.

Lorna interprets signs in feathers, wind, shadows, and flight patterns, and treats campus surveillance as a perfectly respectable branch of prophecy. She has little patience for boorish conversation or careless predictions, though her stern composure softens noticeably in the presence of a particularly fine shiny object.

TeachesOmens, prophecy, aerial observation, symbolic patterns, and divination through wind, feathers, and shadow
Known forUnwanted accuracy, campus-wide awareness, severe elegance, and a suspicious collection of shiny trinkets
Classroom reputationIncisive, exacting, and almost impossible to fool
“The future is never hidden. It is merely ignored by the inattentive.”

Aerial Surveillance

She is not spying. She is interpreting.

If Professor Cawthorne asks where you were last night, the safest assumption is that the ravens have already told her.

Department of Predation

Professor Gideon Bullmaze

Minotaur

Professor Gideon Bullmaze is among the most physically formidable members of the faculty—and one of its most unexpectedly thoughtful.

In confrontation, Gideon is terrifyingly direct, immensely strong, and nearly impossible to stop once committed. Outside combat, he speaks with quiet philosophical gravity, leaving students unsure whether they have attended a hunting lesson or a lecture on life. To Gideon, predation is not blind violence, but purpose, patience, restraint, and the wisdom to know when not to strike.

TeachesTracking, pursuit, tactical restraint, instinct control, pressure under stress, and the psychology of the hunt
Known forImmense strength, calm authority, philosophical observations, and unstoppable commitment
Classroom reputationDemanding, disciplined, surprisingly gentle, and unforgettable when provoked
“A beast that lunges without thought is only hunger. A true predator knows why it moves.”

After the Hunt

Strength without reflection is merely impact.

His students arrive expecting lessons in force. They leave having learned patience, restraint, and several uncomfortable truths about themselves.

Cohort 13 · First-Year Pledge/Legacy Student

Cassandra Gravesong

Sapphire Banshee

Beauty, misfortune, and ancestral arrogance have arrived at Nightshade Academy in the form of Cassandra Gravesong.

Cassandra descends from a Celtic banshee family whose legacy stretches back millennia and entered Nightshade Academy as a Pledge/Legacy student. Vain, snobbish, theatrical, and effortlessly unpleasant, she regards newer monsters and magical creations as unfortunate interruptions in supernatural history. Her manner has left her unwelcome in more fashionable circles, making Cohort 13 the only group that reliably grants her the attention she considers her birthright. Beneath the polished disdain lies genuine power: her voice carries the force of generations, and strong emotion can turn her composure into a devastating spectral outcry.

Cohort 13 · First-Year Standard Admission Student

Winston Fangsworth

Werewolf

Winston Fangsworth is clever, capable, and spectacularly unwilling to remain awake for any of it.

Admitted through the Academy’s standard process on natural ability, Winston has no ancient family pledge or special scholarship behind him. His uniform usually looks as though he slept in it because he usually did. He stays awake far too late baying at the moon, wandering the dormitory halls, and claiming that nighttime is when he does his best thinking. Winston is notorious for dozing through lectures, snoring during examinations, and waking instantly when food is mentioned. His indifference to pedigree makes him the accidental social anchor of Cohort 13, and beneath the laziness he understands far more than his teachers suspect.

Cohort 13 · First-Year Scholarship Student

Tilly Patchwell

Cursed Patchwork Doll

Found abandoned in a castle attic, Tilly Patchwell possesses far more power—and darkness—than her fragile appearance suggests.

Once her abilities were discovered, Nightshade Academy offered Tilly a special scholarship and placed her in Cohort 13. The Academy intends to bring out the formidable monster she was meant to become. Tilly is quiet, sensitive, and convinced she was discarded because too little care went into her creation. She forms deep attachments but expects them to end, initially assuming her cohort-mates tolerate her only because they were assigned together. Her dark magic becomes dangerously unpredictable whenever she feels abandoned.

A room full of companions who cannot leave.

Cohort 13 · First-Year Pledge/Legacy Student

Lucien Brimsmoke

Demon

Fashionable, charming, and naturally mischievous, Lucien Brimsmoke approaches academy life as a game he is certain he has already mastered.

The Brimsmokes are one of the infernal world’s most prestigious demon families, making Lucien a Pledge/Legacy student with every expectation of joining the Academy’s elite social circles. Instead, he prefers Cohort 13—partly from mischief, partly as rebellion against his family’s expectations, and eventually from genuine loyalty. Lucien thinks of himself as exceptionally clever. His actual intelligence is respectable, though not always equal to the elaborate schemes he devises. He routinely overlooks obvious complications, then improvises with such confidence that others briefly assume everything was intentional.
